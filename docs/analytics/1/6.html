Serenity Fund - Risk contribution and Value at Risk

On the graph

The first chart shows the share of the portfolio's total risk carried by each asset, in alphabetical order of ticker. Cboe carries 13.5% of the risk, Philip Morris 12.3%, Eli Lilly 11.5%, Altria 9.8% and Coca-Cola 9.1%. The bond funds SHY, IEF and MUB carry only 0.1% to 0.3% each.

Value at Risk

The second chart shows the maximum loss at a 99% confidence level on a $2 billion investment, from 1 to 10 days (parametric method). It reaches $23.0 million after one day and $61.6 million after ten days, 3.1% of the fund. In other words, there is only a one in a hundred chance of losing more than $62 million in ten days.
