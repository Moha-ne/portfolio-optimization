Serenity Fund - Distribution of daily returns

On the graph

The histogram counts the number of days in each range of daily return. All assets are centred near zero. The bond funds form a very narrow peak, the stocks a wider bell.

Our analysis

A few days are very far from the centre, such as IBM's 29% fall or the +14% days of Microsoft and Apple. These extreme days happen more often than a normal distribution predicts. This matters for Value at Risk: the parametric VaR assumes a normal distribution, so it can underestimate the worst losses.
