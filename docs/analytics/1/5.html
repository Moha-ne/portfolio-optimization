Serenity Fund - Efficient frontier, beta and alpha

On the graph

Each dot is one of the 10,000 random portfolios, coloured by its Sharpe ratio. Their volatility ranges from 5.9% to 11.2% and their return from 14.3% to 23.3% a year. The optimal portfolio has a volatility of 8.40% for a return of 20.50%, a Sharpe ratio of 1.97. The line is the capital market line: it starts at the risk-free rate and touches the cloud at the optimal portfolio.

Beta and alpha against the S&P 500

Beta = 0.26: when the S&P 500 moves by 10%, the fund moves by about 2.6% on average. Alpha = +13.5% a year: the fund earned more than the CAPM predicts for such a low market risk. Both figures come from the course's alpha script (3.py), which uses an equal-weight portfolio of our 25 assets over three years.
