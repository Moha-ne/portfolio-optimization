How we built the universe

Serenity is for cautious clients. As the course recommends, we mixed several asset classes: government and corporate bonds (SHY, IEF, TIP, MUB, VCSH), gold and agricultural commodities (GLD, DBA), strategies with little link to the stock market (DBMF, MNA) and defensive companies: consumer staples (Coca-Cola, Walmart, Altria, Philip Morris), health care (Johnson & Johnson, Eli Lilly, AbbVie), insurance (Travelers, Allstate), utilities (AEP), exchanges and payments (Cboe, Nasdaq, Visa) and a few large tech names (Apple, Microsoft, IBM).

We first picked the assets one by one for their low correlation and positive Sharpe ratio. We then replaced 10 assets, Merck among them, by running the course's Markowitz method on about fifty defensive stocks: we kept the 4 bond funds and a volatility of about 8%, and the Sharpe ratio rose from 1.53 to 1.97.
