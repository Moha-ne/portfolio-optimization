On the graph

The weakest are T-Mobile (114), Berkshire Hathaway (159), Progressive (161) and Apple (192).

How we built the universe

Velocity is for clients who accept more risk, and the team with the best Sharpe ratio earned two bonus points. We tested 58 liquid candidates (technology, finance, industry, energy, gold, commodities) and kept the 25 that give the highest Sharpe ratio with the course's Markowitz method, based on their mean returns and covariance matrix. We left out cryptocurrencies: they also trade on weekends, which drops every Monday from the calculation.

Z-scores

Composite score = 0.6 z(momentum) - 0.2 z(volatility) - 0.2 z(beta), with SPY as the benchmark. As Velocity is a growth fund, we give more weight to momentum (60%) than the course formula (50%) and less to volatility. Micron comes first by far thanks to its momentum. Palantir and Carvana come last: their momentum over the last year is weak and their volatility and beta are very high, but we keep them for the strong returns they bring to the Markowitz portfolio.
