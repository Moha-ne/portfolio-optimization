Velocity Fund - Efficient frontier, beta and alpha

On the graph

The 10,000 random portfolios have a volatility between 14.5% and 24.3% and a return between 30.8% and 48.9% a year. The optimal portfolio has a volatility of 16.88% for a return of 38.37%, a Sharpe ratio of 2.04. The capital market line touches the cloud at this point.

Beta and alpha against the S&P 500

Beta = 1.12: the fund slightly amplifies market moves, which is expected for a growth fund. Alpha = +28.7% a year: the fund earned much more than the CAPM predicts for its level of market risk. Both figures come from the course's alpha script (3.py), using an equal-weight portfolio of our 25 assets over three years.
